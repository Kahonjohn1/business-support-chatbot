## What Is Marque Contact?
Marque Contact is a smart NFC card that combines an identification card and a digital business card.

The product is described as allowing users to share professional information digitally instead of relying solely on paper business cards.

The company profile describes Marque Contact as a live, validated, revenue-generating flagship product.

## Core Offering
- NFC technology integrated into a card
- Customised card design and branding
- Digital sharing of professional details
- A combination of identification and business-card functionality
- Ongoing technical support is listed in the product brochure
