# KB-005 — How Marque Contact NFC Sharing Works

- Source: About Marque Contact Card_Marque Technologies.pdf
- Version: Not stated
- Publication date: Not stated

## How It Works
1. The user taps an NFC-enabled device, such as a compatible smartphone, against the NFC-enabled card.
2. The card transfers the information stored on it.
3. The recipient can access the shared information without manually typing those details.

## Purpose
The product is intended to make professional information sharing faster and more convenient.
