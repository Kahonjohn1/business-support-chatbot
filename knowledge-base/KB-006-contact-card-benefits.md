## Features
- Dual functionality: combines an ID card and digital business card.
- Customisation: supports branding, logos, design, and contact information.
- Digital networking: can share professional information.
- Marketing opportunities: links to websites, social profiles, and promotional materials may be included.
- Reusable format: reduces reliance on repeatedly printing paper business cards.
- Technical support: ongoing technical support is listed as part of the offering.

## Stated Benefits
- Cost efficiency
- Enhanced brand image
- Improved networking convenience
- Increased efficiency when sharing information
- Additional marketing opportunities
- Reduced paper business-card use

## Knowledge Boundaries
These are benefits stated in the brochure, not quantified performance guarantees. Do not claim a specific percentage of savings or business growth.
